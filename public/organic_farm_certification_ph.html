🌿 Organic Farm Certification in the Philippines

Governed by the Bureau of Agriculture and Fisheries Standards (BAFS) under the Department of Agriculture · RA 10068 & Organic Agriculture Act

Philippine National Standard for Organic Agriculture (PNSOA) Two pathways available Annual renewal

Two certification pathways

📋

Third-Party Certification

For larger farms & export markets

1
Choose an accredited certifying body
e.g. Organic Certification Center of the Philippines (OCCP)
2
Submit application
Farm details, land records & input history
3
Develop a Farm Organic Plan
Outline all organic practices; commit to annual reporting
4
Conversion period
2–3 years free of prohibited synthetic inputs
5
Farm inspection
Inspector verifies PNSOA compliance on-site
6
Certificate issued
Valid 1 year · renewed annually
🤝

Participatory Guarantee System

Affordable alternative for smallholders

1
Form or join a PGS core group
Minimum 5 farmer members required (RA 11511, 2020)
2
Apply for BAFS group accreditation
Group must certify under BAFS for PNSOA compliance
3
Peer review process
Members verify each other's farming practices collectively
4
Certification granted
Community-based quality assurance · annual renewal

✔ Lower cost · ✔ Ideal for local markets · ✔ Recognized under RA 11511

Key requirements for both pathways

🚫
No prohibited synthetic inputs (fertilizers, pesticides)
📝
Written Farm Plan & Operations Manual
📊
Farm records: inputs, practices & harvests
🧪
Soil testing to verify compliance
♻️
Approved methods: composting, crop rotation, bio-pest control
🔄
Annual renewal of certification
💰
Third-Party Certification annual cost
Up to ₱70,000/year
PGS is significantly more affordable
